          Drive & capture
          Drive & captureBe the street-view car
          1. Mount your phone facing forward. Dashboard, handlebar or chest, upright or sideways, whichever keeps the lens clear of your bike’s screen. Pick 0.5× for the widest view.
          2. Look both ways at intersections. Stop, turn the handlebars left for 2 seconds, then right for 2 seconds. Waymark notes where the camera pointed (it may ask for motion access), keeps those frames out of street view and labels them for training.
          3. Ride your streets. Video and GPS record nonstop and upload in 2-minute pieces as you go.
          4. Ride each street both ways. Then street view can look in both directions there. The map shows which streets still need the other way.
